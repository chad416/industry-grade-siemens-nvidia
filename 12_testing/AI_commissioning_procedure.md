## Prerequisites

Approved camera/lens/light/enclosure and mechanical datums; representative labeled dataset and approved model card; signed model/configuration bundle; compatible target image; calibrated references; licensed native Siemens project and compiled HMI; approved PKI, network/time services, risk controls and test product disposition.

## Execution

1. Record device serials, firmware, software/container digests, model/dataset/calibration identifiers and certificate fingerprints.
2. Verify power polarity, branch protection, PE/FE/shield termination, network ports and maintenance isolation while de-energized where required.
3. Establish camera working distance and field of view. Lock focus/aperture/mounts; verify exposure, motion blur, glare and per-bottle pixels at maximum speed.
4. Execute dark, flat-field, geometric and reference-target checks appropriate to the selected method.
5. Prove authenticated encrypted OPC UA access with least privilege and correct node ownership.
6. Run normal, boundary and negative challenge images, then every controlled fault-injection case.
7. Verify PLC HOLD/FAULT behavior, first-out diagnostics, immutable acknowledgement and no automatic restart.
8. Measure target latency, throughput, temperature, resource use and dropped frames with representative data; record raw evidence.
9. Back up the approved image, configuration, certificates, calibration and model bundle; perform an isolated restore test.

Commissioning acceptance requires signed project-specific records. This procedure is issued but has not been executed.
