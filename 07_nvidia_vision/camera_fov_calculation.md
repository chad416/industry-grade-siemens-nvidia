# Camera, lens and lighting calculation basis

> FICTIONAL ENGINEERING PROJECT — NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION

Let required horizontal field width be `Wf = 2 × bottle_diameter + nozzle_spacing_margin + 2 × setup_margin`. Use at least 10% extra framing. For sensor width `Ws` and working distance `D`, the thin-lens estimate is `f ≈ Ws × D / Wf` when `D` is much larger than focal length.

No bottle envelope, camera sensor or mounting distance was supplied, so no focal length is released. Depth of field must cover both bottle surfaces and expected misalignment; exposure must freeze conveyor/index residual motion. Backlight area must exceed the entire paired-bottle silhouette. Diffuse front lighting must be independently controlled so damage/spill evidence does not destroy fill-boundary contrast.

Verify with a lens calculator and physical trial across minimum/maximum bottle, fill, product transmission, foam, glare and background conditions. Record pixel-per-millimetre calibration, distortion residual and true fill-reference method.
